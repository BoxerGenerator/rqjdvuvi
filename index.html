A Blox Fruits auto farm script is only as good as its routing. Public farms hard-code one island, so the second you out-level it the XP curve collapses and you babysit the client all night. What "auto farm" means in Blox Fruits

Auto farm is three loops running together: accept the right quest, kill the right mobs fast enough to keep the quest moving, and turn it in before the XP bracket expires. Everything else - raids, chests, fruit spawns - is a side loop that runs when the main queue is empty. The script keeps all three in one scheduler instead of separate toggles fighting each other, which is the usual reason public farms stall at 80% of a level.

How the route is chosen

Every cycle the router reads your level, current sea, equipped fruit and the density of mobs on each island. It scores quests by XP per minute, not by distance, then picks the highest score that your character can actually clear. Dragon and Leopard clear fastest because of their AoE; Kitsune and Dough are the meta picks for single-target; Buddha M1 is the stable fallback when the PC cannot hold high particle counts. If a spot is contested, the script hops to a low-ping server and re-anchors instead of standing in a queue.

Auto quest chain, sea by sea

The quest chain accepts, completes and turns in quests that sit 10 levels above you - the only bracket that keeps XP at the cap. From level 0 to 700 it runs the first sea route and unlocks the portal on foot. From 700 it travels to the second sea, from 1500 to the third. Each transition includes a re-anchor and a verification step, so a failed teleport does not leave the character farming a locked island for six hours.

Auto raid and the race reward

Raids run through the group queue: the script opens the raid, fills the chip slots, clears waves and buys the race reward at the end. The V4 awakening path is the main reason people run raids, and it is fully unattended here. If the group fails to fill, the script backs out, hops, and retries instead of burning the cooldown on an empty lobby.

Fruit sniper, chest ESP and trader labels

The fruit sniper watches spawns across the whole map and buys inside a 400 ms window, which is what separates it from a notifier. It is mythical-first with an ignore list, so common fruits do not eat the queue. Chest, fruit, boss and trader ESP draw through walls with distance and value labels; the same labels appear in the trade window, so you can price a deal without alt-tabbing to a value list.

Money-only mode

If you do not need XP, the money profile skips the quest chain entirely and rotates boss spawns for Beli. Median is 18.2 M per hour with a mid-level account; the route resequences when an event boss enters the rotation. This is the profile people leave running overnight on a second account.
